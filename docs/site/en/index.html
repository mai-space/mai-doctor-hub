Your health record: appointments, reports, medication and symptoms in one place, encrypted on your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play

What the app does

  • Timeline and calendar for every appointment, “report missing” hints and check-ins
  • My record: doctors, diagnoses, symptoms with history, medication, notes and reports with full-text search (PDFs included)
  • Reminders for appointments and medication, widgets for your home screen and watch
  • Optional: “Ask your record” assistant that runs entirely on your device

Your data stays yours

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your entries stay encrypted on your device.
  • Backup and export only when you want, as a file you keep yourself.

All details are in the privacy policy.
